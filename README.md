# Solar Powered Smart Street Lighting System

## Project Overview

The Solar Powered Smart Street Lighting System is an energy-efficient and sustainable lighting solution that uses solar energy, battery storage, sensors, and intelligent control to provide automatic street illumination.

The system reduces dependence on conventional grid electricity and improves energy efficiency through automatic lighting control, motion detection, and dusk-to-dawn operation.

## Objective

To design and develop a solar-powered smart street lighting system that provides reliable illumination while reducing energy consumption, operating costs, and dependence on grid electricity.

## How It Works

Sunlight → Solar Panel → Charge Controller → Battery Storage → ESP32 Control → LED Street Light

The LDR detects ambient light conditions and automatically controls the street light. The PIR sensor detects motion and enables adaptive lighting control to reduce unnecessary power consumption.

## System Workflow

1. Solar panel collects sunlight.
2. Solar energy is converted into electrical energy.
3. The charge controller regulates the generated power.
4. Energy is stored in the rechargeable battery.
5. The LDR monitors ambient light conditions.
6. The ESP32 processes sensor signals.
7. The LED automatically turns ON during darkness.
8. PIR detects motion and enables smart brightness control.
9. The stored solar energy powers the lighting system during nighttime.
